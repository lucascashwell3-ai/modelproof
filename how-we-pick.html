How we pick

Every pick comes from rows you can open. Here is the order.

Step 1

The shortlist

A model joins a task’s shortlist only if somebody independent has said something about it on that task: a tester’s published rank, real spend on OpenRouter, or blind human votes. A benchmark score nobody backs is not enough, and nothing joins for being new or popular. Where no model clears that bar, the row reads no shortlist for this task yet rather than a guess.

Step 2

Your filters cut it

Your plans decide which labs you reach. Your data rule drops a whole vendor; a country we could not confirm is never a match. Never-suggest models never enter an automatic pick.

Step 3

Your stance orders it

Models are grouped by how much of that evidence puts them near the top of the field, best group first. Best takes that order as it stands. Cheapest takes the lowest price inside the best group that still clears the evidence bar — never a cheaper model from a weaker group. Balanced keeps everything priced at or under twice the cheapest qualifying model, and ranks the rest after. The stance also sets the effort we write into AGENTS.md. It reorders the shortlist; it never cuts it.

Step 4

The evidence

Three kinds, kept apart and never averaged: independent testers’ ranks, spend share on OpenRouter, and blind human votes. Agreement across more of them beats agreement across fewer. Alongside them: list price per million tokens, and one headquarters country per vendor with a source. Nothing else counts, and no model is ranked by us.

Step 5

What we never do

Never invent a number. Never favour a lab. Never propose what your rule or your list rules out, even when it is cheaper. Never hide a cost’s assumption.

Sources

The site’s own files

Prices, tags and scores come from data/models.json and data/plans.json — the same files every other page on this site reads. Vendor countries sit in data/vendors.json, one source each.
