Rule order

Reachable with what you have → clears the data rule → clears the task's judged capability floor (a real judgment call, not a benchmark number alone) → ranked by judged band, then confidence, then stance, ties broken by price. A preview-status or low-adoption pick can lose the top spot to a same-band alternative. See assets/decide.mjs for the exact order.
